Who We Are

Dog Paw is a non-profit origanisation established in 2020 during covid 19 lockdown by a group of animal lovers

Our Mission

Our mission is to support dogs and their owners by providing essential care, resources and assistance.

Our Goal

We aim to reach more dog lovers and expand our support to communities in the West Rand and beyond.
