Welcome to Dog Paw

Dog Paw is a non-profit organisation dedicated to helping dogs and supporting owners who may struggle to afford essential care.

Our goal is to make sure that dogs receive the food, comfort and care they deserve.

Get Help Get Involved

What We Do

Dog Food

We provide essential food support to dogs whose owners may be struggling financially.

Dog Essentials

We assist with items such as beds and food and water dishes to improve dogs' wellbeing.

Medical Support

We help owners access assistance with important medical care when possible.
